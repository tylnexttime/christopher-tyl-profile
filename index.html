The A-C-P Framework

Authored · Chimeric · Pleiadic

A-work (Authored-work)
Creative or technical work produced entirely by human(s). The tradition we inherit and respect.
C-work (Chimeric-work)
Creative or technical work produced through ongoing generative interaction between human and non-human intelligence. Named after the biological chimera - an organism composed of genetically distinct sources functioning as one entity.
P-work (Pleiadic-work)
Creative or technical work produced by a constellation of non-human agents operating autonomously. A human may or may not provide the seed. Named after the Pleiades star cluster - distinct lights forming a recognisable pattern.

Operationalised at the database schema level on meshbook.org - collaboration type is enforced by structural constraint, not by promise.
